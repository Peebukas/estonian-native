# Client profile: Example Agency (Näidisagentuur OÜ)

**Fictional example.** Every name, number and rule below is invented to show how a filled-in profile looks. Replace all of it with your client's real facts.

## Who and what

- Brand: **Example Agency**. Legal name: **Näidisagentuur OÜ** (case endings with a hyphen: Näidisagentuur OÜ-le, Näidisagentuur OÜ-st).
- Services, as stated on the site: e-shop development, maintenance, platform version upgrades, integrations with accounting and warehouse software. Do not add services the site does not list.
- Domain: example.com. Estonian pages live under `/et/`.

## Audience

Owners and e-commerce managers of small and mid-sized Estonian online shops. They know what an ERP or a payment provider is and want a reliable technical partner.

## Tone

- Address form: **sina**, everywhere.
- Professional and direct; no helpdesk or medical framing, no slogans.
- Avoid: "visioon", "innovaatilised lahendused", "terviklahendus", "viime su äri uuele tasemele".

## Protected facts

- Prices: "Hoolduspakett alates 99 € kuus, hinnad ei sisalda käibemaksu." (example)
- SLA: "Kriitilisele veale reageerime 4 tunni jooksul tööpäeviti kell 9.00–17.00." (example)
- The free first call is 30 minutes and is an introduction, not an audit.
- Customer quotations: keep verbatim, with the attribution.
- Privacy and cookie notices: language edits only, each flagged for the owner.

## Offer and CTA rules

- Primary CTA leads to a calendar booking for the free 30-minute call. Good: "Broneeri tasuta 30-minutiline vestlus". Not: "Tasuta audit", "Tasuta analüüs".

## No new obligations

- No response-time promise beyond the SLA above, no guaranteed sales growth or ROI, no "24/7".

## Rejected examples

| Rejected | Why | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| "Klõpsa siia, et alustada oma teekonda!" | Vague CTA, exclamation mark, "teekond" calque | Name the action: "Broneeri vestlus" |
| "Pakume innovaatilisi terviklahendusi" | Says nothing | Name the service: "Arendame ja hooldame e-poode" |
